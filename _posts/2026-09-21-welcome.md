---
title: "Welcome: why I'm starting this blog"
---

Most of what I think about as a scientist never makes it into a paper: half-formed hypotheses,
analysis tricks that took a week to figure out, questions I can't answer yet.
This blog is where those go.

Expect posts on:

- **Astrocytes and blood flow**: what we see when we image them in awake, behaving mice
- **Analysis methods**: MATLAB pipelines, whisking and locomotion tracking, statistics for messy biological data
- **Doing science**: notes from conferences, papers I'm reading, and life as a PhD student

Thoughts and disagreements are welcome. Email me at [fangyuanlicla@gmail.com](mailto:fangyuanlicla@gmail.com).
